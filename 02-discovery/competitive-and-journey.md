# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Quality-focused streaming subscribers who value finding a great movie or show quickly, but become frustrated when a large catalog makes discovery feel overwhelming and time-consuming.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Find a great movie or show to watch in minutes, so they can spend their limited leisure time enjoying content rather than searching through an overwhelming catalog.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** After a long day, the user opens StreamLine looking for something great to watch, spends 20 minutes scrolling through thousands of options without confidence in any choice, and ultimately leaves the platform to seek recommendations elsewhere because finding something worth watching requires too much effort.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Google search ("best movies to watch", "hidden gems", "top films this week")
Review and rating sites (IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Letterboxd)
Friends and family recommendations (text messages, social media, group chats)
Competitor streaming services with curated recommendation emails or hand-picked selections
Online articles and "what should I watch tonight?" lists
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Open StreamLine and browse through recommendations and categories for 15-20 minutes without finding anything compelling.
2. Leave the app and search Google for movie recommendations, reviews, or "what to watch tonight" lists.
3. Check review sites or ratings to validate whether a title is worth their time.
4. Ask friends, family, or online communities for recommendations they trust.
5. Return to StreamLine with a specific title in mind or choose content on a competing platform instead.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The process feels most broken when the user realizes they have spent 15-20 minutes browsing thousands of titles and still have less confidence about what to watch than when they started, forcing them to leave StreamLine and rely on Google, review sites, friends, or competing services to make the decision for them. This turns entertainment into research and defeats the purpose of the platform.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** "I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There's 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want." (UXR-01)

Additional supporting evidence:

"Honestly I just re-watch the same four comfort shows. Discovering anything new feels like a chore, so I don't." (UXR-03)

"Three of six participants said the sheer number of choices made them anxious and they'd rather someone 'just tell me what's good tonight.'" (UXR-12)
