# GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard (Module 6)

## Get your prior work ready
- **The feature you're launching from your M4 PRD, one feature, not a list:** Spotlight Curated Rail: A home-screen rail displaying 5-10 editor-curated recommendations immediately upon app launch to help users find something worth watching within 5 minutes and reduce choice overload
- **What your M5 experiment told you shipped / iterating, the evidence behind the launch:** Ship if successful. The experiment tested whether Spotlight increases the percentage of sessions where users start watching within 5 minutes of opening StreamLine. The predefined shipping criterion was a +5 percentage point improvement in watch-start rate at p < 0.05, while maintaining content completion, recommendation satisfaction, and session-length guardrails. The hypothesis was that curated recommendations would help users choose content faster and start watching sooner.
- **Your persona pull your M2 persona, this anchors your audience:** Quality-focused streaming subscribers who want to find a great movie or show quickly but become frustrated when large catalogs make discovery overwhelming and time-consuming. These users often spend 15-20 minutes browsing before making a decision or leave StreamLine to seek recommendations elsewhere.

## Set your goal, then your audience
- **Primary GTM goal awareness · engagement · conversion, pick one:** Engagement. Spotlight Curated Rail is designed to change user behavior after they open StreamLine. The feature is not primarily about acquiring new users (awareness) or driving purchases (conversion). Its purpose is to reduce browsing time and help users start watching content faster. Success is measured by increasing the percentage of sessions that start playback within 5 minutes of app launch.
- **Why this goal? what makes this the right goal for this feature right now:** Spotlight Curated Rail is designed to help existing subscribers find content faster and start watching sooner. The success metric is increasing the percentage of sessions that begin playback within 5 minutes of app launch, making engagement the primary objective.
- **Target audience the specific segment your goal implies, be precise:** Existing quality-focused StreamLine subscribers who experience choice overload when browsing large content catalogs and want trusted recommendations that help them quickly decide what to watch.

## Size your launch
- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** L (Multi-Channel)
- **Justification reach + revenue impact + what silence would risk:** Spotlight affects a broad segment of existing StreamLine subscribers and addresses a core user experience problem: content discovery. The feature is intended to change a key behavior, increasing the percentage of sessions that start watching within 5 minutes of app launch. Because it directly impacts engagement and retention, the business impact is meaningful. If StreamLine ships the feature silently, many users may never notice or understand the value of the new curated experience, reducing adoption and limiting the opportunity to improve watch-start behavior.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** Launch.

This feature changes how users discover content and represents a new experience on the home screen. Users need awareness and context to understand why Spotlight exists and how it helps them solve choice overload. A simple product release would risk low adoption, whereas a focused GTM launch can drive discovery, trial, and engagement with the feature.

## Choose your top three channels and plan assets
- **Channel 1 owned / earned / paid, and why it reaches your audience:** Owned: In-App Promotion

This is the highest-leverage channel because the target audience is existing StreamLine subscribers already using the product. Spotlight appears on the home screen, making in-app messaging the fastest way to drive awareness and trial among users experiencing choice overload.

Email reaches existing subscribers who may not open StreamLine daily. The campaign can reinforce the value proposition: spend less time browsing and start watching faster with trusted curated recommendations.

## Define how you'll know it worked
- **Success metrics 2 to 3 metrics that match your GTM goal:** 1. Watch Starts Within 5 Minutes (Primary Metric)

Measure: % of sessions where users start watching content within 5 minutes of opening StreamLine.
Target: +5 percentage point improvement versus baseline.

2. Spotlight Engagement Rate

Measure: % of users who click a title from the Spotlight Curated Rail.
Indicates whether users find the curated recommendations compelling enough to explore.

3. Spotlight-Initiated Watch Starts

Measure: Number or percentage of watch starts originating from Spotlight recommendations.
- **Bad signal to watch for e.g. high reach, zero signups = message-market mismatch:** Bad Signal #1

High Spotlight impressions but low click-through rate.
Interpretation: Users see Spotlight but do not find the recommendations relevant or compelling.

Bad Signal #2

High Spotlight clicks but no improvement in watch starts within 5 minutes.
Interpretation: The recommendations are attracting interest but failing to help users make faster viewing decisions.

Bad Signal #3

Watch starts improve, but content completion rate or recommendation satisfaction declines.
Interpretation: Users are starting content faster but selecting lower-quality or less suitable content.
- **Most likely post-launch decision double-down · iterate · pivot · deprioritize, and what would trigger it:** Double-Down

Trigger:

Watch starts within 5 minutes improve by at least +5 percentage points.
Content completion rate remains within guardrails.
Recommendation satisfaction remains within guardrails.
Spotlight engagement is strong.

Iterate

Trigger:

Positive movement in watch-start rate, but improvement is below the +5 percentage point threshold.
Strong engagement with Spotlight but limited impact on the primary metric. This would suggest improving curation quality or placement rather than removing the feature.
