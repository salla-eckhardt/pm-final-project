# GTM Launch Plan, StreamLine (B2C)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Spotlight Curated Rail: A home-screen rail that displays 5-10 trusted editor-curated recommendations immediately upon app launch, helping quality-focused streaming subscribers reduce choice overload and find something worth watching within 5 minutes. |
| Goal | Awareness |
| Launch tier | L, Multi-channel |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Awareness, Spotlight Curated Rail is designed to improve how existing StreamLine subscribers discover content and move more quickly from browsing to watching. The feature addresses a proven engagement problem: users spend 15-20 minutes searching, often leave the platform without selecting content, and rely on external recommendation sources. The goal is to deepen usage by increasing confident content selection and reducing discovery friction.
- **Target audience:** Quality-focused streaming subscribers who want to find a great movie or show quickly but become frustrated when a large catalog makes discovery overwhelming and time-consuming.

These users:

Spend 15-20 minutes browsing before choosing content.
Often leave StreamLine to use Google, IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Letterboxd, friends, or competing services for recommendations.
Want trusted guidance rather than endless catalog browsing.
Value finding something worth watching quickly so they can spend their limited leisure time enjoying content instead of researching it.

## Launch Tier
- **L, Multi-channel**, Spotlight Curated Rail addresses one of StreamLine's most significant user problems: content discovery and choice overload. User research shows many subscribers spend 15-20 minutes browsing, often leave the platform without watching anything, and seek recommendations from Google, review sites, friends, or competing services.

The feature affects a broad segment of existing subscribers and is intended to improve engagement, retention, and viewing behavior by helping users find something worth watching faster. Research also identified discovery failure as a major product health issue, making this more than a minor UI enhancement.

A silent release would risk low awareness and adoption. Users may not understand that the new curated rail exists or why it helps them, limiting the impact on watch-start rates and engagement. The feature warrants coordinated in-product promotion, email communication, and supporting content, but does not require a full XL launch with major press events.

## Channels
1. **Owned: Your audience is already inside StreamLine. Spotlight appears on the home screen, so in-app banners, tooltips, and launch messaging are the most direct way to drive awareness and adoption among subscribers experiencing choice overload.**
2. **Owned: Many users who struggle with discovery may not open StreamLine every day. Email allows StreamLine to re-engage existing subscribers and communicate the benefit: spend less time searching and start watching faster with curated recommendations.**
3. **Earned: Users already seek recommendations from external sources such as reviews, articles, and recommendation lists. Editorial coverage reinforces Spotlight's value as a trusted discovery experience and helps build credibility around StreamLine's curated approach.**

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** 1. % of sessions where users start watching within 5 minutes of opening StreamLine (Primary Metric)
 Target: +5 percentage point improvement versus baseline. This is the success metric defined in both the PRD and experiment.

2. Spotlight Engagement Rate
 Percentage of users who click a recommendation in the Spotlight Curated Rail. This measures whether users are engaging with the new discovery experience.

3. Spotlight-Initiated Watch Starts
 Percentage of watch starts that originate from a Spotlight recommendation. This validates that Spotlight is driving actual viewing behavior, not just clicks.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** High Spotlight engagement but no improvement in watch starts within 5 minutes.  This would indicate users are noticing and clicking Spotlight recommendations, but the recommendations are not helping them make faster viewing decisions. In that case the issue is likely recommendation quality or curation rather than awareness.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Iterate  Trigger: Spotlight engagement is positive, but the watch-start improvement is below the +5 percentage point threshold needed to justify broader investment. Improve recommendation quality, placement, or curation strategy before expanding the feature.
