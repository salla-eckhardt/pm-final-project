# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** Quality-focused streaming subscribers
- **Primary metric:** % of sessions where a user starts watching within 5 minutes.
- **Moment of misery:** "I scroll for twenty minutes and close the app without watching anything." Users leave StreamLine and seek recommendations elsewhere.
- **Guardrail:** Content completion rate and recommendation satisfaction. Improvements in quick-start rate must not reduce viewing quality or user trust in recommendations.

| A1 Spotlight Curated Rail | 5 | 2 | Quick Win | Now | Directly reduces choice overload with trusted curation. |
| A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label | 4 | 2 | Quick Win | Now | Builds trust in recommendations and reduces decision paralysis. |

| A8 Watch Party (Spotlight) | 1 | 5 | Time Sinker | Cut | Solves social viewing, not content discovery. |
| A9 Advanced Filter Engine | 2 | 4 | Time Sinker | Cut | Treats symptoms; overwhelmed users want guidance, not more filters. |

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, Directly reduces choice overload with trusted curation.
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, Builds trust in recommendations and reduces decision paralysis.
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, Helps users discover quality content quickly with minimal effort.

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, Aligns with how users choose entertainment after a long day.
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, High discovery impact but requires significant personalization work.

### LATER, backlog
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, Supports retention but does not solve in-session discovery.

### ✂ Cut List
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, Adds engagement but weak connection to the core misery.
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, Solves social viewing, not content discovery.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, Treats symptoms; overwhelmed users want guidance, not more filters.
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, Improves consumption after selection, not selection itself.
