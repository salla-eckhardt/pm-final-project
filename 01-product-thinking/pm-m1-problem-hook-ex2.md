## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine Spotlight. I am committing to the streaming platform scenario focused on improving content discovery through curation.
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If StreamLine does nothing for the next 12 months, engagement will continue to stagnate as users increasingly view the platform as a large but undifferentiated content warehouse. Competitors with stronger recommendation and curation experiences will attract quality-focused viewers, weakening StreamLine's market position and customer loyalty.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** A user finishes work and wants to watch something great, but spends 20 minutes scrolling through endless options without confidence in what to choose. They leave StreamLine and search Google, ask friends for recommendations, browse review sites, or switch to a competitor with better content discovery.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** As StreamLine's massive catalog creates decision fatigue instead of value, quality-focused viewers are abandoning the platform because finding something worth watching requires too much effort.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Spotlight transforms StreamLine from a content warehouse into a trusted entertainment guide by delivering curated collections and personalized recommendations that help viewers discover high-quality content faster. Launching now is urgent because engagement growth has stalled and competitors are winning users through better discovery experiences rather than larger catalogs.

Plateauing engagement
Perception as a "warehouse"
